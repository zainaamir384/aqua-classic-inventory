# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
# Aqua Classic Water Filters Inventory System

## 1. Goal
Provide a streamlined, high-aesthetic inventory management platform tailored for water filter manufacturing, retail sales, and component tracking in Pakistan (PKR currency).

## 2. Key Modules
- **Dashboard & Analytics:** Live KPI cards for SKUs, Stock Value (PKR), Sales Revenue, Net Profit, and interactive 7-day Sales & Profit line chart.
- **Product Catalog:** Manage products under 13 clean categories.
- **Custom Unit Assembly:** Manually type custom assembled unit names (e.g. 5-Stage Custom RO Plant) and produce stock.
- **Stock Ledger (Option A):** Read-only transaction log with Category, Type, and Search filtering.
- **Sales Logging:** Log sales to Walk-in Customers or Salesmen with custom unit sale prices.
- **Owner Profile:** Profile dashboard at `/accounts/profile/` for managing account stats and security.
